# LazyRemote 1.9 beta 1 (iPhone)

## What's new

- A quick, short swipe on the trackpad no longer sends the page flying. Scrolling now coasts after you lift your finger in proportion to how far you swiped, like a MacBook trackpad.
- Long, deliberate swipes still coast as far as before, and moving the cursor is unchanged.

## Mac app

No changes. Version 1.5 of the Mac app works with this release.
