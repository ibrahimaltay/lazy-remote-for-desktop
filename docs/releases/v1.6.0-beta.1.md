# LazyRemote 1.6 beta 1 (iPhone)

## What's new

- A redesigned Spotlight search. Tap the magnifier at the top and the screen dims and blurs behind a floating search bar.
- Type what you want and tap "go". It is entered on your Mac and the top result opens.
- Tap the dimmed area, or tap "go" with nothing typed, to close Spotlight.
- If your search could not be sent, you see "Not confirmed" and nothing is opened by mistake.
- The magnifier is easier to see and fades when you are not connected.
- The text bar at the bottom now always sends plain text, so it no longer switches into a Spotlight mode.
- VoiceOver can open Spotlight and close it with a "Close Spotlight" action.

## Mac app

No changes. Version 1.5 of the Mac app works with this release.
