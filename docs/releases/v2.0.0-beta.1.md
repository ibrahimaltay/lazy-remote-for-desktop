# LazyRemote 2.0 beta 1 (iPhone and Mac)

## What's new

- Spotlight search on your iPhone now shows the results your Mac is showing. As you type, a list of the top results appears under the search bar and updates as the Mac refines them.
- Your typing appears in Spotlight on the Mac as you type, so the results follow along live.
- Text left in Spotlight on the Mac from earlier is cleared when you open search on your iPhone.
- The results list is for reading only. You cannot tap a result yet.

## Known issues

- If you close Spotlight on your Mac, search stays open on your iPhone and the two get out of sync. This will be fixed in a later release.

## Mac app

This release needs both apps updated. Install Mac app version 2.0 and iPhone app version 2.0 together; the new Spotlight list does not work with older versions of the Mac app.
