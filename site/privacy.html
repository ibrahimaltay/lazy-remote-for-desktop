Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

LazyRemote does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking. The app contains no third-party SDKs.
  • Bluetooth. The iPhone app uses Bluetooth only to connect directly to your own Mac and send remote-control input (button presses, pointer movement, text). This traffic is end-to-end encrypted and never leaves your devices or passes through any server.
  • On-device storage. Pairing keys are stored in the Keychain, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. They are never transmitted to us.
  • Children. The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

This website uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party resources.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
