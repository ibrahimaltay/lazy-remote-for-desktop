Getting started

Install

  1. Download LazyRemote.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag LazyRemote into the Applications folder.
  3. The iPhone app will be on the App Store soon.

Connect

  1. Open LazyRemote on your Mac and allow Bluetooth.
  2. Click the LazyRemote icon in the menu bar, select Open Accessibility Settings, and turn LazyRemote on.
  3. Open LazyRemote on your iPhone and allow Bluetooth.
  4. On your Mac, click the menu bar icon and allow your iPhone.
  5. A green dot next to your Mac's name means you are connected.

Troubleshooting

Buttons do nothing on the Mac

LazyRemote needs Accessibility access. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Accessibility, and make sure LazyRemote is on. If it already is, turn it off and on again, then restart LazyRemote.

The iPhone can't find the Mac

Make sure Bluetooth is on for both devices and both apps have Bluetooth access. Check that LazyRemote is running in the Mac menu bar. Then on the iPhone, tap the ••• menu and select Reconnect.

The connection is refused after reinstalling

For security, LazyRemote refuses a device whose identity has changed. On the iPhone, select Forget Mac in the ••• menu. On the Mac, select Remove next to the iPhone in the menu bar. Then pair again.

Still stuck? Ask on GitHub.

Privacy

We collect nothing.

LazyRemote does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.

The iPhone app uses Bluetooth only to talk to your own Mac. Everything it sends is encrypted and never leaves your devices.

This website uses no cookies or analytics.
